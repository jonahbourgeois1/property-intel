Delivery

Vendor polygons live in ai/original/regions.json and never change. Edits are saved as a diff to data/nearmap/edits/ when opened from the sheet, or to local ai/edits/regions.json. Revert regions returns to original.

Pins editor. Regions are shown read-only (finish them in the Regions editor). Seed makes one pin per region at its interior point — no vegetation, Lawn Grass excepted. Place pin adds one by hand. Save writes the Nearmap sheet only.
