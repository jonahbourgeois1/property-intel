Every property has a story. The view from above helps tell it.

This is a professional aerial mapping of the property that requested it — not neighborhood surveillance. The drone is a measuring tool, the modern equivalent of a survey, so camera placement, lighting, wildfire readiness, and emergency access can be planned from a perspective that is hard to see from the ground.
