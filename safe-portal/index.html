What this mock shows

One SAFE map per property. Security · Access · Fire · Emergency service are the four layers of a single record, drawn on the Google satellite canvas the customer touches. The report is the entry point; the map is the product.

Live Device Canvas. Cameras with per-camera cones, sensors, access points with unlock, fire devices and alerting devices — whoever installed them. An alarm event flies to the device and shows what is around it.

Vendor-agnostic. Devices from CHEKT, a competitor's alarm, a gate contractor and an irrigation vendor share one map. Vyanet does not need the monitoring contract.

Placement legend. Solid ring = surveyed (GPS still, sheet pin, record pin). Dashed = estimated or mock for this walkthrough. Nearmap imagery appears only in the reports, never on this canvas.
