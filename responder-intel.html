How it works

Overhead imagery turned into operational intelligence.

The Nadir Property Intelligence system analyzes straight-down aerial imagery and extracts two categories of structured data: Property Elements — the physical features visible on the property — and Responder Considerations — site characteristics that affect access, movement, visibility, staging, and operations. The objective is situational awareness, not inspection or assessment.

🛰
Nadir Aerial Imagery
Straight-down overhead imagery captured from drone, satellite, or orthographic aerial photography — optimized to show the full property footprint at the right zoom level.
📍
Property Elements
Clearly visible physical features — buildings, driveways, parking areas, storage, fencing, pools, and more — labeled from an approved vocabulary and geo-pinned on the map.
🚒
Responder Considerations
Observable site characteristics that affect access, navigation, visibility, and staging — single access points, dense vegetation, outdoor storage, limited rear access, and similar conditions.
🔥
Wildfire Conditions
Fuel continuity, defensible space, vegetation adjacent to structures, and access route conditions — identified from imagery and mapped for suppression and evacuation planning.
Who it serves

Built for the people who respond first.

Property Intel is designed for the agencies and professionals who need to understand a property before reaching it. The system does not estimate value, assess maintenance conditions, evaluate code compliance, or predict hazards. It answers one question: what would a responder see from above?
