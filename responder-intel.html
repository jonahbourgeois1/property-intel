What your report contains

Overhead imagery turned into operational intelligence.

Every Vyanet property report extracts two structured data categories: what exists on the property and what first responders need to know. The objective is situational awareness — not inspection or assessment.

Imagery
Nadir Aerial View
Straight-down drone imagery optimized for the property footprint — every structure, driveway, and access point visible from above.
Elements
Property Elements
Buildings, driveways, fencing, pools, storage — labeled from an approved vocabulary and geo-pinned on a live satellite map.
Access
Responder Considerations
Observable site conditions affecting access, navigation, visibility, and staging — single access points, dense vegetation, limited rear access.
Wildfire
Wildfire Conditions
Fuel continuity, defensible space, and vegetation adjacent to structures — mapped for suppression and evacuation planning.
