Method

   

How these numbers were produced

   
   

Measurements come from the 2026-07-02 aerial capture.     Scale was fixed by Nearmap ground sample distance, verified against the in-frame Nearmap scale bar at 5 zooms (within 0.5%).

   

Counts of bunkers, patches and stalls were made by eye across that imagery.     Canopy cover is a point-intercept estimate. Where tree shadow or resolution     prevented a reliable count, the value is left blank rather than estimated.

   
Full source and method notes      

Capture date 2026-07-02 (Nearmap vertical and oblique; date label read on every frame used). Units: areas in acres, lengths and distances in feet, shares in percent. Scale anchor: Nearmap ground sample distance GSD = 156543.03392 x cos(lat) / 2^zoom m/px, verified against the scale bar Nearmap draws inside the same frames at 5 zooms (z19 50 ft = 71 px, z19.5 20 ft = 40 px, z20 20 ft = 57 px, z20.5 20 ft = 80 px, z21 10 ft = 57 px; bar/formula ratio 0.997-1.005, within 0.5%). Frame centre = pixel (640, 328) of each 1280x800 capture, confirmed by feature matching two same-centre frames at z19 and z20 (scale 0.5000, fixed point 638.8, 327.8). At lat 44.08: z18 1.407 ft/px, z19.5 0.498 ft/px, z20.5 0.249 ft/px, z21 0.176 ft/px. Imagery-derived values in this file: residence distance_to_turf (pixel distance from roof edge to nearest mown turf in the residence vertical, x GSD, +/-10 ft), all frame descriptions and counts, canopy-contact and falling-distance calls. Course-wide values come from a 30-frame z18 Nearmap vertical mosaic (2026-07-02; frames cropped x 70-1180, y 70-580, placed by Web Mercator position and checked by SIFT matching, 4648 x 3108 px at 1.407 ft/px). Per-frame histogram matching was tested and not applied: tile overlaps in the raw mosaic already matched (mean colour difference 0.08) and matching raised it to 8.0. Envelope = mosaic turf plus water mask grown by 100 ft (208.6 acres); extent_ns and extent_ew are its bounding extents in feet. irrigated_turf_area = turf mask inside the envelope (colour and texture classes, lit and shaded, morphological clean-up); it reads low where tree shadow covers turf. turf_patch_count = connected turf patches of 1,000 sq ft or more inside the envelope; largest_patch_share = largest patch as a percent of that patch total. canopy_cover_pct = point-intercept photo-interpretation of 250 random points inside the envelope (72 canopy, 28.8%, standard error about 2.9 points). Water acres = mosaic water mask with ponds 2 and 3 traced by hand where tree shadow blocked the mask; ponds 1 to 5 = 1.18, 1.19, 0.81, 0.49, 0.13 acres (3.80 total). bunkers_total = 44 on-course bunkers plus 1 practice bunker, counted by eye across the mosaic in panels after a sand-colour candidate pass. parking_stalls = striped stalls in the main clubhouse lot, counted along each row in clubhouse vertical AG_CLB_02 (z19.5) with the lot rotated to the row axis: west row 33 north of the hatched aisle and 10 south of it, east row 40, plus 4 accessible stalls; shaded runs were counted from the stripe pitch (about 14.5 px, roughly 7 ft), so the count carries about plus or minus 5. The north end of the same lot is confirmed in AG_CLB_04; the small clubhouse drop-off court is not striped in the imagery and is not included. path_length_per_hole, structures_within_50ft_of_turf, canopy_by_zone and the grey crown count stay null (see cannot_see). Cross-check against OSM (not used for any value): water 4.48 acres across 5 polygons (imagery 3.80, 85%; per pond 1.51, 1.36, 0.73, 0.71, 0.18); 48 bunker polygons (imagery 45, 94%); OSM extent 6,722 ft N-S (OSM polygons include the practice ground and northern holes past the mosaic envelope's buffered turf edge, imagery 5,607 ft); OSM largest playing-surface share 10.4% measures separate fairway, green and tee polygons, so it does not compare with the connected-turf share; 18,807 ft of golf=cartpath lines (missing holes 1, 8, 9, 18). Earlier detection-layer work (834 patches, largest 56%) was searched for on the box and not found; the imagery count differs because it uses a 1,000 sq ft floor (1,220 components with no floor) and joins turf across narrow shaded gaps. Image fields are null where no Cloudinary upload exists; tee, path and residence entries without any uploaded image were removed and the rest renumbered (residences 01-06 were 01, 02, 04, 08, 09, 10; paths 01-06 were 01, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08). Residences keep the captured order and were not re-ranked: frame-centre distance to the mosaic turf mask (62, 166, 104, 132, 192, 120 ft for AG-RES-01 to 06) measures from the pin, not the roof edge, and the mask misses turf under shadow, so it did not pass as a ranking basis. distance_from_clubhouse = great-circle distance from the Mothership Golf tab column K 'Green N' pin to the 'Clubhouse' pin, in feet.
